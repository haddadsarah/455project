How CipherBreak works

The journey

  1. Sign in — your progress is saved to your account.
  2. Start an adventure in Guided mode (full tutorials, free hints, practice) or Challenge mode (shorter instructions, recorded as an independent result).
  3. Intro & lobby — the lab locks down; NOVA explains the mission.
  4. Four rooms, unlocked in order: {% for n, r in rooms.items %}{{ r.name }}{% if not forloop.last %} → {% endif %}{% endfor %}.
  5. Escape and review your results by learning objective.

The first time you meet each operation, an interactive overlay highlights the controls, explains why the operation matters, asks you to perform it or answer a tiny practice question, and only then lets you continue. You can replay any tutorial.

A challenge never scores below 0. Badly formatted answers (wrong length, non-hex characters) are explained and never cost points. Points are awarded once per challenge. Speed is never scored; an optional timed challenge only displays your time.

Controls

Everything works with mouse, touch or keyboard: Tab moves between controls, Enter/Space activates, and in tutorials → = next, ← = back, Esc = skip. Settings let you mute sound, reduce motion and enlarge text.
