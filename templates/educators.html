Teach the cipher in motion.

Follow the values behind the DES diagrams. CipherBreak EDU gives students practice with fictional data and gives instructors a tool for live demonstrations and classroom assessment.

A 25-minute classroom activity

  1. 5 min · Set the baseline. Open the optional pre-assessment and discuss the difference between the block width and effective key length.
  2. 5 min · Walk through the Hex Gate and Key Vault. Build a 64-bit block, follow IP, and demonstrate PC-1, rotating C and D, and PC-2.
  3. 10 min · Investigate the Reactor and Decryption Chamber. Demonstrate the S-box lookup, inspect a Feistel round and trace the reversed decryption keys.
  4. 5 min · Compare and discuss. Use the post-assessment to review the same outcomes and explain the 56-bit key limitation. Assign a full mission for independent practice.

Live demonstration
